Firmware Updater

Update your device to the latest firmware directly from this page — no Arduino IDE, no extra software.

Before you start

  1. Plug the device into your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge — this won't work in Firefox or Safari, they don't support the browser feature this relies on.
  3. Click Install below, then pick the device's port when your browser asks (it'll usually show up as something like "USB-SERIAL CH340" or similar).
Your browser doesn't support this — please open this page in Chrome or Edge instead. This page needs to be loaded over HTTPS to flash a device.

This updates your device's firmware.
A bad connection or unplugging mid-update won't damage the device — if something goes wrong, just reconnect and try again.
